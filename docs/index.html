HOW IT WORKS

// 4,800 samples per frame. no pixels were harmed.

1

THE SPIRAL

Each frame is scanned by two interlaced spiral arms that morph from a circle to a 4:3 rectangle. Left channel rides arm A, right channel rides arm B.

2

FM LUMA

Brightness is pitch: black = 3 kHz, white = 12 kHz. FM laughs at tape hiss and level wobble.

3

AM CHROMA

Colour is loudness: U on the left, V on the right. A calibration chirp in every frame teaches the decoder your tape's treble loss.

4

1 kHz SYNC

A beep starts every frame. A flywheel clock keeps 10 fps steady even when the tape stretches, drops out or is pure noise.
